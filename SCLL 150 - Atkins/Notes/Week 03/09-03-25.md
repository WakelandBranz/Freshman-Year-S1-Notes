## Compacts vs. Covenants: Key Notes

## **Similarities**

- Both are formal agreements between parties
- Create binding obligations and commitments
- Require mutual consent to establish
- Can be legally enforceable

## **Key Differences**

**Compacts:**

- Primarily **governmental/political** agreements
- Often between states, regions, or public entities
- Focus on practical cooperation (trade, resources, infrastructure)
- More **transactional** in nature
- Examples: Interstate water compacts, regional transportation agreements

**Covenants:**

- Broader application: **legal, religious, or personal**
- Can involve private parties, communities, or spiritual commitments
- Often include moral/ethical dimensions
- More **relational** or sacred in character
- Examples: Homeowners association rules, biblical covenants, loan agreements

## **Context Matters**

- **Legal field**: Covenants often appear in contracts (restrictive covenants in real estate)
- **Political field**: Compacts typically govern inter-jurisdictional cooperation
- **Religious field**: Covenants carry spiritual significance and ceremonial weight

**Bottom line**: Compacts are usually about practical governance between public entities, while covenants can be legal, personal, or sacred agreements with broader moral implications.
