### **Delegate Model**

- **Definition**: Representatives act strictly according to their constituents' wishes.
- **Key Idea**: "Voice of the people."
- **Role**: They see themselves as messengers, not decision-makers.
- **Example**: Voting based on polling or town hall feedback, even if they personally disagree.

---

### **Trustee Model**

- **Definition**: Representatives use their own judgment to make decisions, even if it goes against public opinion.    
- **Key Idea**: "Act in the public’s best interest, not just their immediate demands."
- **Role**: They are trusted to use knowledge and experience to make tough calls.
- **Example**: Voting for a controversial law they believe is ultimately beneficial.

---

### **Politico Model**

- **Definition**: A hybrid of the delegate and trustee models.    
- **Key Idea**: Flexible approach depending on the issue.
- **Role**: Sometimes follow constituents, sometimes act independently.
- **Example**: Acting as a delegate on high-profile issues, but as a trustee on complex or low-interest matters.
