### Articles of Confederation
##### Weaknesses
 - **No power to tax** – The federal government couldn't collect taxes, relying on states to contribute funds voluntarily.
    
- **No national army** – There was no standing army to defend the country or handle internal unrest.
    
- **Weak central government** – Most power was held by the states, making national coordination difficult.
    
- **No executive branch** – There was no president or central leadership to enforce laws.
    
- **Unanimous consent for amendments** – All 13 states had to agree to amend the Articles, making change nearly impossible.

**The constitution was signed on September 17th**
