##### Constitution - ===The government and way of life of a civilization/city===

### Athenian Civilization
- Anyone can hold office
- Rotation of officers
- Active participation in government from citizens
- Freedom of speech
- Liberal/relaxed educational system
- Athenian citizens feared and respected the law
- Social mobility; you are not locked into the social hierarchy which you were born in

> Socrates was put to death.
> 	He wanted to find out who was the most intelligent person in Athens so he went around questioning people about their intelligence. In the process he asked politicians and revealed their lack of intelligence thus making them angry so they put him to trial. In said trial he was put to death despite Athens' freedom of speech.

### Spartan Constitution
- Mixed constitution
- Council of elders (gerousia)
	- Council of 28, over the age of 60, they are on the council for life
	- Sort of an aristocracy... the elders are highly qualified in their role
	- It is suspected that these elders were chosen democratically
		- Chosen by a group of individuals who shouted their name the loudest

### Spartan Lifestyle
- From the time you were 7 you were guaranteed a comprehensive education system and an effective fitness program
- Guaranteed land after your service (you were likely to die in war prior to this though)
- Your life is set as long as you survive your mandatory service
- You couldn't choose your partner if you were a woman
- Lycurgus (the founder of Sparta) made wealth completely undesirable thus making nobody pursue it
- Eugenics was extremely prevalent in Spartan culture. 
	- Children who were born with disabilities were almost guaranteed to be sent away or killed.
		- This is known as infanticide
- Treatment of slaves was especially horrible
