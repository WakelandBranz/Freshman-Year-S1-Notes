##### Constitution - ===The government and way of life of a civilization/city===

### Athenian Civilization
- Anyone can hold office
- Rotation of officers
- Active participation in government from citizens
- Freedom of speech
- Liberal/relaxed educational system

> Socrates was put to death.
> 	He wanted to find out who was the most intelligent person in Athens so he went around questioning people about their intelligence. In the process he asked politicians and revealed their lack of intelligence thus making them angry so they put him to trial. In said trial he was put to death despite Athens' freedom of speech.
