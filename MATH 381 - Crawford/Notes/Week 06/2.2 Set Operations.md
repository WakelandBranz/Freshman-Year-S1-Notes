## Definitions

### Union

> **Definition:** Let $A$ and $B$ be sets. The *union* of the sets $A$ and $B$, denoted by $A \cup B$, is the set that contains those elements that are either in $A$ or in $B$, or in both.
> 
> **Notation:** A ∪ B **In words:** An element $x$ belongs to the union of the sets $A$ and $B$ if and only if $x$ belongs to $A$ or $x$ belongs to $B$. This tells us that: 
> $A \cup B = \{x \mid x \in A \lor x \in B\}$
>
> The union of the sets $\{1, 3, 5\}$ and $\{1, 2, 3\}$ is the set $\{1, 2, 3, 5\}$; that is, $\{1, 3, 5\} \cup \{1, 2, 3\} = \{1, 2, 3, 5\}$

---

### Intersection

> **Definition:** Let $A$ and $B$ be sets. The *intersection* of the sets $A$ and $B$, denoted by $A \cap B$, is the set containing those elements in both $A$ and $B$.
> 
> **Notation:** A ∩ B **In words:** An element $x$ belongs to the intersection of the sets $A$ and $B$ if and only if $x$ belongs to $A$ and $x$ belongs to $B$. This tells us that $A \cap B = \{x \mid x \in A \land x \in B\}$

---

### Disjoint

> **Definition:** We say two sets $A$ and $B$ are disjoint in $A \cap B = \emptyset$.
> 
> **Key idea:** NOTHING is shared between the two sets (or the empty set is shared????)

### Set Difference

> **Definition:** The difference of two sets $A$ and $B$ is $A - B := A \ B := \{ x \mid x \in A \land x \not \in B \}$. This is sometimes referred to as the "Complement of $B$ in $A$."
>
> In general, given a set $A$, and universe set $U$, the complement of $A$, denoted $\overline A$ or $A^\complement$, is $\overline A := \{  x \mid x \in U \land x \not \in A \}$.
