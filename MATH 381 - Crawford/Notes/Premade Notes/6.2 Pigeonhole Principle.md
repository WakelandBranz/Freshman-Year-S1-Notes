## Thm: (Pigeonhole Principle)

---

## Cor:

A function $f : A \to B$ where $|A| = n$ and $|B| = m$ cannot be injective.

---

## Ex 1.

(a) In a room of 367 people, at least 2 of them will share a birthday.

(b) In a list of 27 English words, at least two of them will begin with the same letter.

(c) How many students are needed in a class to be sure that at least two people get the same exam grade?

---

## Thm: (Generalized Pigeonhole Principle)

---

## Ex 2.

(a) Among 100 people, at least of them are born in the same month.

(b) A minimum of how many students are needed to ensure at least 6 people get the same letter grade?

(c) How many cards need to be drawn to ensure at least 3 from the same suit have been chosen?

(d) In a state with 25 million telephones, how many area codes are needed to ensure that every phone has a distinct phone number?

---

## Ex 3.

Show that among any $n + 1$ positive integers not exceeding $2n$ there must be an integer that divides one of the other integers.

---

## Thm:

Every sequence of $n^2 + 1$ distinct real numbers contains a subsequence of length $n + 1$ that is either strictly increasing or strictly decreasing.
