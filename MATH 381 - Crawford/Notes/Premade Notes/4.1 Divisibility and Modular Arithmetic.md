## Theorem

For integers a, b, c, a ≠ 0:

(1) If a | b and a | c, then a | (b + c).

(2) If a | b, then a | bc for any integer c.

(3) If a | b and b | c, then a | c.

---

## Corollary

If a, b, c are integers (with a ≠ 0) such that a | b and a | c, then a | mb + nc for some m, n ∈ ℤ.

## Definition: Congruence modulo m

---

## Theorem

Let a, b, m ∈ ℤ with m > 0. Then a ≡ b(mod m) iff a mod m = b mod m.

---

## Theorem

Let m > 0 be an integer. The integers a ≡ b(mod m) iff there exists k ∈ ℤ so that a = b + km.

---

## Theorem

Let m > 0 be an integer. If a ≡ b(mod m) and c ≡ d(mod m), then

(i) a + c ≡ b + d(mod m)

(ii) ac ≡ bd(mod m)

---

## Corollary

Let m > 0 be an integer and let a, b ∈ ℤ. Then

(i) (a + b) mod m = ((a mod m) + (b mod m)) mod m

(ii) (ab) mod m = ((a mod m)(b mod m)) mod m
