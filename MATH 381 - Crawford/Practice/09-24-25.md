If it is raining, then the ground is wet

$p \rightarrow q$
$\lnot p \lor q$
$p \land \lnot q$

It is raining, and the ground is not wet.

#### Modus Ponens
$\begin{align} p &\rightarrow q \\ &p \\ \hline &\therefore q \end{align}$

### Modus Tollens
$\begin{align} p &\rightarrow q \\ &\lnot q \\ \hline \therefore &\lnot p \end{align}$

#### Hypothetical Syllogism
$\begin{align} p &\rightarrow q \\ q &\rightarrow r \\ \hline \therefore p &\rightarrow r \end{align}$

#### Disjunctive Syllogism
$\begin{align} p &\lor q \\ &\lnot q \\ \hline \therefore &p \end{align}$

#### Addition
$\begin{align} &p \\ \hline \therefore p &\lor q \end{align}$
#### Simplification
$\begin{align} p &\land q \\ \hline \therefore &p \end{align}$

#### Conjunction
$\begin{align} &p \\ &q \\ \hline \therefore p &\lor q \end{align}$
#### Resolution
$\begin{align} p &\lor q \\ \lnot p &\lor r \\ \hline \therefore q &\lor r \end{align}$
