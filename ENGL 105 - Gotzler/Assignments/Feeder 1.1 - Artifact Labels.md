Go to an online digital collection of historical and/or cultural artifacts (e.g., the Ackland Art Museum’s online collection database [https://ackland.org/collections/)](https://ackland.org/collections/) [Links to an external site.](https://ackland.org/collections/).  Browse the artifacts and each select a piece which interests you. Study the object and create a label  that would accompany the piece on display. This label should include metadata information as well as a thematic interpretation and an explanation of any  relevant historical or cultural details. It might be helpful to go to the different exhibitions the local museums such as the Ackland or Wilson Library have on display and look at some examples of labels to get a feel for the style and kinds of information included.  These labels should contain a "tombstone" header with essential details about the artifact (e.g., artist, title of work, date, medium) and should be readable by a visitor casually wandering through an exhibit; therefore, a good length is between 75-150 words.
