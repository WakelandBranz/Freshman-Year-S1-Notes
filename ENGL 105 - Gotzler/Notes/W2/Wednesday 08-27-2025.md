### Review of Conceptual Foundations
- **Genres** are typified responses to recurrent rhetorical situations
- **Rhetorical situations** have component elements and contextual elements
- **Contextual elements**: The discourse communities involved and the sites of social action that genres facilitate
- We can *leverage* our **knowledge of the rhetorical situation** to glean practical insights about genres that we use

#### Genre Awareness in the Humanities
- Center the human experience
- Evaluative questions
- Textual evidence/archives and special collections
- Close reading, theoretical and historical interpretation, performance and creative practice
