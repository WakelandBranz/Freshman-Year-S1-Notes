## Narrative
- Narrative is not serious whatsoever; the irony of the fact that 'Octodad' is an octopus is highlighted by the fact that other characters seem either unaware or don't care.
- Unserious character placed in semi-serious scenarios to fit the theme while maintaining the unserious tone
- Gameplay elements are highlighted by the narrative (octopus man experiencing daily human life)
- Mundane narrative is made comedic by gameplay elements
