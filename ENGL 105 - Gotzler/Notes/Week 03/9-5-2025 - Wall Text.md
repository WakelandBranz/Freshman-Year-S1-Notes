
### Wall Text Should...
1. **Transmit Insights**
	- offer interpretive/aesthetic analysis
2. **Inspire Interest**
	- provide historical/cultural information 
3. **Communicate Choices**
	- situate objects within a curatorial argument
---
#### *General Guidelines*
- speak to the viewer and the art *simultaneously*
- effect and intellectual but generous tone
- employ an active voice and use short sentences

#### *Triangular Composition Strategies*
- 'the 3-way switch'
- general question--specific answer--general theme
- audience who knows: the same--less-and more

#### *Aspirational Goals of the Genre*
- observation before explanation
- motivate/direct further thought/action
- ASK: *Why is this profound and exciting?*
