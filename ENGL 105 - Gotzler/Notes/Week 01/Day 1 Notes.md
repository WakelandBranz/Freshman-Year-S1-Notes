° Learning objectives
	Moving from "rules for writing" to flexible writing practices informed by rhetorical theory
	Moving from broadly applicable research models to gaining multi-disciplinary awareness and hands-on research experience
	Moving from writing your typical 5 paragraph essays to composing across a range of genres and modalities
	Modeling academic and professional habits of working collaboratively through peer review and critical feedback

- The rhetorical situation refers to the complex of factors which writers respond to with any composition
	- In simpler terms, the rhetorical situation is the set of circumstances that influence how and why a writer creates a piece of writing.
- *Component Elements of Rhetorical Situation*
	- **Genre
	- X Post, YouTube, etc.
	- **Audience**
		- The group that any genre seeks to address
	- **Role**
	- **Response**
- *Contextual Elements of Rhetorical Situation*
	- **Discourse Communities** (role; audience)
	- **Sites of social action** (genre, purpose)
