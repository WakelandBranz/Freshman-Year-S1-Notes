### What is a hypothesis test?
 - Statistical inference is used to make decisions or judgments about the value of a population parameter based on a sample statistic
 - Hypothesis tests can be used to answer specific questions based on sample information.
 - Hypothesis tests require a null and alternative hypothesis.

---
### Establishing the Hypothesis
 - The **null hypothesis** $(H_0)$ is what we currently believe to be true about the population
	 - Historically accepted values
	 - Most recent information available
	 - Status quo
- The alternative hypothesis $(H_a)$ is what we are trying to evaluate based on the sample data that we have collected
	- "Do the data show that..."
	- Challenge the null hypothesis
---
### Example
A snack-food company produces a 454-gram bag of pretzels. Although the net weights deviate slightly from 454 grams and vary form one bag to another, the company insists that the mean net weight of the bag must be kept at 454 grams. As part of its program, the quality assurance department periodically performs a hypothesis test to deicde whether the packaging machine is working properly. That is, they need to decide whether or not the mean weights of the bags is different from 454 grams.

What are the null and alternative hypotheses?

 - $H_0: \text{ The mean weights of the bags is 454 grams.}$
 - $H_a: \text{ The mean weights of the bags is different from 454 grams.}$
