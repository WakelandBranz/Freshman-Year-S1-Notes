## Data visualization

### Nominal Data Visualization methods
- Bar charts and pie charts are typically used to represent nominal (name) data.
	- We will NOT be using pie charts though. They are difficult to read and there are better options that accomplish the same thing.

### Bar Charts
 - Visual representation of the frequency distribution of a categorical (nominal or ordinal) variable.
 - One bar for each of the unique values of the variable
 - Height of the bar is the count or percent of observations in that category.

#### How to create a bar chart
![[Pasted image 20250922144927.png]]

```python
plt.figure()
sns.barplot(x=lot_count.index, y=lot_count.values)
plt.ylabel('Number of Homes')
plt.xlabel('Lot Configuration')
plt.xticks(rotation=20)
plt.title("Distribtuion of Lot Configuration)
plt.show() # Creates the graph
```

#### Distributions
 - Distributions can be represented visually by
	 - Histogram
	 - Box Plot
- Distributions are described by
	- Center
	- Spread
	- Location
	- Skewness

#### Histogram
 - Displays the distribution of a numerical variable
 - Data values are placed into equal-sized bins that are
	 - Distinct and non-overlapping
	 - Defined by their lower bounds (inclusive)
 - Seaborn will automatically choose the bin width based on the range of the data being displayed
 - Height of the bar represents the number of observations in the bin
	 - Alternatively, you can normalize the values in the histogram such that the height of the bar represents the proportion (relative frequency) of observations in the bin

#### Measures of Center: Mean
 - Most important measure of location
 - Computed as the simple average for all of the data values of a variable
 - **Preferred measure of center for data with symmetric distributions**
 - **Very sensitive to outliers**
#### Measures of Center: Median
 - Values such that 50% of the observations are greater than the median and 50% of the observations are less than the median (when the observations are arranged in ascending order)
 - Also known as the $50^{th}$ percentile
 - **Preferred measure of center when dealing with data that is highly skewed or that have extreme values (outliers**

#### Measures of Center: Mode
 - Most common occurrence in a data set
