## Data visualization

### Nominal Data Visualization methods
- Bar charts and pie charts are typically used to represent nominal (name) data.
	- We will NOT be using pie charts though. They are difficult to read and there are better options that accomplish the same thing.

### Bar Charts
 - Visual representation of the frequency distribution of a categorical (nominal or ordinal) variable.
 - One bar for each of the unique values of the variable
 - Height of the bar is the count or percent of observations in that category.

#### How to create a bar chart
![[Pasted image 20250922144927.png]]

```python
plt.figure()
sns.barplot(x=lot_count.index, y=lot_count.values)
plt.ylabel('Number of Homes')
plt.xlabel('Lot Configuration')
plt.xticks(rotation=20)
plt.title("Distribtuion of Lot Configuration)
plt.show() # Creates the graph
```
