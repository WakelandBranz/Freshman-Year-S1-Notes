
**Population**: The group of ALL items of interest to a statistics practitioner.
 - Frequently very large; sometimes. infinite
 - Described by ***parameters***
**Sample**: A set of data drawn from the population
 - Potentially very large, but less than the population
 - Described by ***statistics***

---
### Non-probabilistic Sampling Methods
 - Convenience sampling is selecting samples that are easily accessible
	 -  Ask your friends or whoever walks by
- Volunteer sampling is only having self-chosen participants
	-  Social media polls
- Judgmental sampling is selecting samples based on the examiner's best judgment
	- Use only results you think are relevant, ask only experts, focus groups
