## What is Artificial Intelligence?

In the broadest sense, Artificial Intelligence (AI) applies advanced analysis and logic-based techniques to interpret events and to support and automate decisions and actions (Gartner, What is Artificial Intelligence?)

**Generative AI:** systems that can create new content by learning from patterns in data

**Predictive AI:** systems based on machine learning models that are designed for specific tasks, such as classification, prediction, or decision making based on structured data (IBM, What is Predictive AI?)

---

## Predictive AI

- Designed to leverage historical data to perform specific prediction tasks efficiently
- Uses supervised and unsupervised machine learning algorithms
- Relies primarily on structured data
- Results in better decisions, cost savings, and increased profitability

## Key Takeaways

- Always begin with the end in mind!
- What is the problem you are trying to solve?
- How will you know if you are successful?
- Don't underestimate the "data preparation" phase
- Machine learning is an iterative process
- You're not done until the model is deployed!
- Don't forget about monitoring and maintenance!
