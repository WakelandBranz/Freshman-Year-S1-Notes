### Why visualize data?
- Difficult for the human eye (people) to understand or extract patterns and trends from large tables.
- Graphs can convey a lot of information in an interpretable manner

##### A graph should:
- Stand on its own (self-explanatory)
- Be tailored for a specific purpose
- Use the simplest plot appearance
- Avoid visual clutter (colors, designs)
- Use appropriately scaled and labeled axes
- Remove gridlines and chart borders
- Use symbols and lines that are distinct and readable
- Use color to convey information BUT make sure that it's still interpretable in black and white.
