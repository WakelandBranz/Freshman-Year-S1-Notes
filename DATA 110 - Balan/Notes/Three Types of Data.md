**[Nominal Data](app://obsidian.md/Nominal%20Data)**

- **What it is:** Categorical data where values are names or labels only.
- **Key trait:** No inherent order or ranking.
- **Examples:**
    - Colors (red, blue, green)
    - Gender (male, female, non‑binary)
    - Country codes (US, FR, JP)
- **Typical uses:** Grouping, counting frequencies, creating bar charts.

**[Ordinal Data](app://obsidian.md/Ordinal%20Data)**

- **What it is:** Categorical data with a clear, meaningful order.
- **Key trait:** The distance between categories is _not_ quantified.
- **Examples:**
    - Survey ratings (Strongly disagree → Strongly agree)
    - Education levels (High school < Bachelor < Master < PhD)
    - Medal rankings (Gold, Silver, Bronze)
- **Typical uses:** Ranking items, median/percentile calculations, non‑parametric tests.

**[Interval Data](app://obsidian.md/Interval%20Data)**

- **What it is:** Numerical data where differences between values are meaningful, but there is no true zero point.
- **Key trait:** Ratios are not interpretable (e.g., 20 °C is not “twice as hot” as 10 °C).
- **Examples:**
    - Temperature in Celsius or Fahrenheit
    - Calendar years (1990, 2000, 2010)
- **Typical uses:** Calculating means, standard deviations, and performing parametric statistical tests.

_Quick tip:_

- **Nominal →** just names.
- **Ordinal →** ordered names.
- **Interval →** ordered numbers with equal spacing, but no absolute zero.
